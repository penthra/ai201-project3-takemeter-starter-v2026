# CasualConversation taxonomy; baseline.py reads these definitions.
# AI-proposed labels and annotations; human review is pending.
# Detailed boundary rules and examples are in LABEL_REVIEW.md.

personal_experience: mainly shares the writer's experiences, personal circumstances, or feelings; a generic closing invitation does not override a self-contained personal account
discussion_request: mainly asks readers for answers, stories, advice, judgments, recommendations, or participation; a specific unresolved request can outweigh a long background story
viewpoint: mainly presents or explains a broader opinion, observation, recommendation, or idea; a brief moral at the end of a personal story is not enough
